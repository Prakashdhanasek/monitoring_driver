Driver Monitoring System (DMS)

Step-by-Step Live Demo Presentation Script (Automatic Trip End Workflow)

Demo Execution Plan
Phase 1: Verified Driver Flow (Registered Driver in DB)
STEP 1 Face Verification & Driver Recognition
  • Action: Align a registered driver's face in front of the camera on the Verification Screen.
  • Mechanism: FaceAuthEngine matches facial embeddings with local/backend DB embeddings.
  • Result: Displays "Driver Verified" indicator, plays chime, and retrieves driver profile.
🗣️ What to Say during Demo:
"First, when a registered driver sits in the vehicle, the system performs real-time face authentication using local facial embeddings. The system successfully recognizes the driver and starts the trip session."
STEP 2 Live Monitoring & Telemetry Streaming
  • Action: App navigates automatically to the Live Monitoring Panel.
  • Mechanism: Processing ~30 FPS camera feed. TelemetryService initiates live frame streaming to dashboard/server.
STEP 3 Triggering & Demonstrating Incident Types
👁️ Drowsiness Alert
PERCLOS > 30% or Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) drops. Audio warning chime plays.
🚨 Wake Up / Sleeping Alert
Eyes closed >= 5s OR Head Drop >= 5s. Loud audio "WAKE UP!" alarm + screenshot capture.
📱 Phone Usage Detection
Cell phone detected in hand/ear via ObjectDetectorEngine. Visual alert triggered.
🚬 Smoking Detection
Cigarette object classified near mouth/hand. Audio & visual alert triggered.
📐 Out of Boundary / Head Distraction
Yaw angle > 25° for sustained 3s or face moves outside designated camera box. "Driver Distracted" banner displays.
STEP 4 Automatic Trip Completion (No Manual Button)
  • Automatic Trigger 1 (Stationary Speed): When vehicle speed remains ≤ 6.0 km/h for 30 seconds (stationary/parked), trip auto-completes.
  • Automatic Trigger 2 (No Driver/No Face): When driver leaves vehicle (no face detected for 30 seconds), trip auto-completes.
  • Result: Sends _sendTripEnd() telemetry and automatically transitions back to the Verification Screen.
🗣️ What to Say during Demo:
"There is no manual Stop Trip button required. The trip ends 100% automatically when the vehicle stays stationary (≤ 6 km/h) for 30 seconds or when the driver exits the vehicle, safely resetting to the Verification Screen."
Phase 2: Unverified / Unknown Driver Flow (Person NOT in DB)
STEP 1 Unknown Driver Detection
  • Action: Unregistered person sits in front of camera.
  • Result: Face authentication fails to match DB → Tagged as "Unknown Driver / Unauthorized Access".
STEP 2 Incident Monitoring & Security Alerting
  • Action: System auto-starts trip under UNKNOWN driver profile.
  • Triggering Incidents: Test all 5 incidents (Wake up, Drowsiness, Phone, Smoking, Out of Boundary). All telemetry sent to server tagged with security alert.
STEP 3 Automatic Trip Completion (Unknown Driver)
  • Action: Person steps away / vehicle stops (≤ 6 km/h for 30s) → System automatically finishes session → Returns to Verification Screen.
Phase 3: Vehicle Speed Trigger Flow (≥ 20 km/h) & Frame Check
STEP 1 Automatic Monitoring Activation (≥ 20 km/h)
  • Action: Vehicle speed exceeds 20 km/h (via GPS or accelerometer).
  • Mechanism: System automatically bypasses waiting state and forces transition to active monitoring.
🗣️ What to Say during Demo:
"If the vehicle begins moving above 20 km/h before manual verification completes, the system automatically forces active monitoring to guarantee continuous driver safety."
STEP 2 Frame Reception Check
  • If Frames Received: Bounding box renders, EAR/YAW calculations run, incident alerts activate normally.
  • If Frames NOT Received: System flags camera frame drop / video stream disconnection error.
STEP 3 Automatic Reset
  • Action: Vehicle stops ($\le 6$ km/h for 30s) or no frames/driver $\rightarrow$ Auto-completes trip session $\rightarrow$ Resets to Verification Screen.
